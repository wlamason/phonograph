# Design and mechanical references

Research consulted on 12 November 2026. All modelling, site code, grain textures, vector illustrations, and compositions in this repository are original; no reference-site code or assets were copied.

## Interaction research

- [ExplodeView interactive viewer](https://explodeview.com/): direct orbit controls, reversible assembly animation, part highlighting, and labels. Applied as a continuous slider plus a one-action explode/reassemble control. An independent component index makes the same information available without precise 3D pointing.
- [University of Washington: Automated Generation of Interactive 3D Exploded View Diagrams](https://grail.cs.washington.edu/projects/exview3D/exview3D-SIG08.pdf): exploded diagrams should preserve meaningful assembly relationships. Applied as a vertical stack along the platter/bearing axis, with lateral separation of the pickup and motor and dashed attachment guides.
- [Sculpting Harmony](https://www.uicoach.io/inspirations/award-winning/sculpting-harmony): an exhibition framing for exploring a technical subject through 3D media. Applied as a single focused volume, with editorial typography, measured pacing, and substantive explanations.
- [Apple: Get started with the HTML Model Element](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2026/215/): 3D as directly manipulable content with named views. Applied as free orbit plus a reliable camera reset. Three.js was chosen for broad browser support and independent control of exported Blender parts.

## Mechanical references

- [Rega Planar 3](https://www.rega.co.uk/products/planar-3) and [quick-start guide](https://www.mueller-spring.ch/media/pdf/rega/planar3/planar-3-qsg-final.pdf): belt, subplatter, motor pulley, bearing, tonearm and external phono connections. Our deck is an original generic design, with an aluminium platter and walnut cabinet; it is not represented as a Rega product.
- [What Hi-Fi: The making of the Rega Planar 3](https://www.whathifi.com/features/making-rega-planar-3): practical assembly relationships, bearings and tonearm details.
- [Ortofon: What is a moving-magnet cartridge?](https://ortofon.com/pages/what-is-moving-magnet/): a magnet attached to the cantilever moves beside fixed coils.
- [Ortofon: Cartridge design](https://ortofon.com/pages/cartridge-design-with-leif-johannsen): stylus, cantilever, suspension and generator principles.
- [Audio-Technica cartridge guide](https://manuals.sterremuur.nl/Brochures/DT%20Guides/AT%20cartridgeguide.pdf): the stereo cartridge and pivoted tonearm signal path.

## Refinement, twice

1. **Core:** pull a modern turntable apart and identify the components.
2. **First refinement:** preserve assembly axes, attach labels to real geometry, offer a six-moment guided tour, make every control reversible, and explain the external amplification chain.
3. **Second refinement:** give the experience a listening-room atmosphere with original local music, gentle gain ramps, a pickup that physically meets the LP, speed-linked pitch changes, quiet labels in the assembled view, locally hosted editorial type, subtle material highlights, reduced-motion support, and a usable fallback when 3D is unavailable.

The illustrations explicitly distinguish educational simplification from physical scale. Audio is a listening-room accompaniment; it is not claimed to be generated by a simulated groove or cartridge.
